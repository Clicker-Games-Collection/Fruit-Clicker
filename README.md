## How to Play

Your adventure begins by building a lush garden filled with vibrant fruits. Click to gather resources and use your earnings to purchase upgrades that enhance your production capabilities. Unlock new fruits and juice-making processes to maximize your output and earn gold for further expansion.

## Key Features

- **Stunning Graphics**: Enjoy beautiful visuals and lively animations that bring your farm to life.
- **Diverse Upgrades**: Choose from over 50 unique upgrades, each designed to boost your farm’s efficiency.
- **Variety of Fruits**: Unlock and cultivate 7 different types of fruits to diversify your production.
- **Rebirth Mechanics**: Experience exciting rebirth options that grant powerful improvements for your next playthrough.
- **Laboratory Access**: Open a lab to explore dozens of studies that enhance your farming strategies.
- **Competitive Leaderboards**: Compete against other players and climb the leaderboards to showcase your fruit-farming skills.
